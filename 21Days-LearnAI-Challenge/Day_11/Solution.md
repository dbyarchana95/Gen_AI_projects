# My Solution

### Tool Used : Resume.io

### Steps Followed:
1. Visit https://resume.io
2. Sign up or log in to your Resume.io account.
3. Click **Create Resume**.
4. Choose **Start from Scratch** or import an existing resume.
5. Select a professional resume template.
6. Enter your personal and contact information.
7. Write a professional summary.
8. Add your work experience.
9. Include your education details.
10. Add your technical and soft skills.
11. List your certifications.
12. Showcase your projects and achievements.
13. Add languages and any additional sections (optional).
14. Review your resume for accuracy, grammar, and formatting.
15. Preview the final resume.
16. Download your resume in PDF format.
17. Customize the resume for each job application before submitting.
