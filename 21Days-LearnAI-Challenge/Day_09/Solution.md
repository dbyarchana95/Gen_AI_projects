# My Solution

### Tool Used : Framer AI

### Steps Followed:
1. Sign up for a free account on Framer.
2. Click **Create New**.
3. Select **Generate with AI**.
4. Enter your portfolio website prompt.
5. Click **Generate**.
6. Review the generated website.
7. Replace the placeholder content with your personal information.
8. Update the Hero section.
9. Add your About Me section.
10. Add your Skills section.
11. Add your Experience section.
12. Add your Projects section.
13. Add your Certifications section.
14. Add your Resume download button.
15. Add your Contact section.
16. Replace placeholder images with your own.
17. Customize colors, fonts, and icons.
18. Update the navigation menu.
19. Add links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and email.
20. Check the desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
21. Optimize the page title and meta description.
22. Preview the website.
23. Publish using the free Framer subdomain.
24. Share your portfolio website link.
25. Update the website regularly with new projects and achievements.
