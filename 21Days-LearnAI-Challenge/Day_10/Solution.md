# My Solution

### Tool Used : suno.io

### Steps Followed:
1. Open **https://suno.com** in your web browser.
2. Sign up or log in to your Suno account.
3. Click the **Create** button.
4. Choose **Simple Prompt** or enable **Custom Lyrics** if you have your own lyrics.
5. Enter your song prompt or paste your lyrics.
6. Describe the desired music style, genre, mood, vocals, and instruments.
7. Click **Create** (or **Generate**) to generate the song.
8. Wait for Suno to create one or more song versions.
9. Listen to the generated songs and compare the results.
10. Refine your prompt or lyrics if needed and generate another version.
11. Select your favorite version.
12. Download or share the final song (depending on your plan).

### Prompt to ChatGPT to write lyrics
```commandline
Create an uplifting motivational pop song about time management. The song should tell the story of someone who wastes time, realizes that every second is precious, and transforms their life through planning, discipline, and consistent action. Include a memorable chorus that encourages listeners to value every moment. Build from a reflective beginning to an energetic, inspiring finale. Use emotional vocals, piano, acoustic guitar, cinematic drums, and an anthemic finish. metaphor to weave it throughout the song:
"Every tick is an opportunity."  
concept: "Beat the Clock"
An energetic motivational song.
Clock ticking throughout the music.
Theme:
Stop waiting.
Start doing.
Build momentum.

with this concept, write lyrics for the song
```
