###  Steps to Generate a QR Code for Your Restaurant Menu

1. Open QR code generator website (https://qrcodecreator.com/).
2. Sign in .
3. Upload your restaurant menu PDF.
4. Select **PDF** as the QR code content type.
5. Click **Generate QR Code**. 
6. Download the QR code in your preferred format:
    - PNG (Digital use)
    - SVG (High-quality printing)
    - PDF (Professional printing)
