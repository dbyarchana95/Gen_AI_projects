# My Solution

---
## Task : writing email
### Tool Used : ChatGPT

### Prompt
```commandline
Act as a professional business communication expert.

Write a formal email to a bank requesting a change to my registered email address. The email should:

Have a clear and professional subject line.
Politely explain that I want to update my registered email address.
Mention my old email address as oldemail@example.com.
Mention my new email address as newemail@example.com.
Request the bank to update its records and confirm once the change has been completed.
Include a polite closing and professional tone.

After writing the email:

Review it for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and clarity.
Identify any weaknesses or areas for improvement.
Rewrite the email with improved wording, professionalism, and readability.
Explain the changes you made and why they improve the email.
Give the improved version a professionalism score out of 10.
```

### Output:
```commandline

I hope you are doing well.

I am writing to request an update to the email address registered with my bank account. I would like to replace my current registered email address with a new one so that I continue to receive all account-related communications, transaction alerts, and important notifications without interruption.

Current Registered Email Address: [oldemail@example.com](mailto:oldemail@example.com)

New Email Address: [newemail@example.com](mailto:newemail@example.com)

I kindly request you to update your records with my new email address at your earliest convenience. Please let me know if you require any additional documents or verification to process this request.

I would appreciate it if you could confirm once the email address has been successfully updated.

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to your confirmation.

Yours faithfully,

[Your Full Name]
[Account Number (Last 4 Digits): XXXX]
[Registered Mobile Number]
[Customer ID (Optional)]
```
