# My Solution

### Tool Used : Google Docs

### Steps Followed:
1. Open your web browser.
2. Go to https://docs.google.com.
3. Sign in to your Google account.
4. Click **Blank Document** to create a new document.
5. Enter a title for your short story.
6. Write or paste your short story into the document.
7. Format the text (headings, font, spacing, alignment, etc.).
8. Review and proofread your story.
9. Add images or illustrations (optional).
10. Google Docs automatically saves your work.
11. Click **Share**.
12. Choose the appropriate sharing permissions (Viewer, Commenter, or Editor).
13. Copy the shareable link if you want others to read it.
14. If needed, download the document as PDF, DOCX, or another format.
15. Share the document link or downloaded file with your audience.
