# My Solution

### Tool Used : Canva

### Steps Followed:
1. Sign in to your Canva account.
2. Click **Create a Design**.
3. Search for **Website**.
4. Select a **Website** template or start with a blank design.
5. Choose a fitness or gym-themed template.
6. Customize the homepage with your gym name and logo.
7. Add a hero banner with a motivational headline.
8. Insert a call-to-action button (Join Now, Book a Free Trial, Contact Us).
9. Create an **About Us** section.
10. Add a **Services** section (Personal Training, Group Classes, Yoga, Cardio, Strength Training, etc.).
11. Add a **Membership Plans** section.
12. Include a **Class Schedule** section.
13. Add a **Meet Our Trainers** section with photos and descriptions.
14. Showcase gym facilities using high-quality images.
15. Add customer testimonials and success stories.
16. Include a photo gallery or video section.
17. Add a **BMI Calculator** or fitness tips section (optional).
18. Create a **Contact Us** section with phone number, email, and address.
19. Add a Google Maps location link (optional).
20. Include links to your social media profiles.
21. Customize colors, fonts, and icons to match your gym branding.
22. Preview the website on desktop and mobile views.
23. Review all content and fix any errors.
24. Click **Publish Website**.
25. Choose a free Canva domain or connect a custom domain (if available).
26. Share your gym website URL.
