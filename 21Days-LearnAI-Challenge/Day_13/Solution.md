# My Solution

### Tool Used : Framer AI 

### Steps Followed:
1. Sign in to your Framer account.
2. Click **Create New**.
3. Select **Generate with AI**.
4. Enter a prompt describing your book landing page.
5. Click **Generate**.
6. Review the AI-generated layout.
7. Replace the placeholder title with your book title.
8. Add the book subtitle (optional).
9. Upload your book cover image.
10. Write a compelling book description.
11. Add key features or benefits of the book.
12. Include a section highlighting what readers will learn.
13. Add author information and a professional photo.
14. Insert testimonials or reader reviews (optional).
15. Add a "What's Inside" or chapter overview section.
16. Include a strong call-to-action (Buy Now, Pre-order, Download Sample).
17. Add purchase links (Amazon, personal website, etc.).
18. Include a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section (optional).
19. Add your social media and contact information.
20. Customize colors, fonts, icons, and animations to match the book's theme.
21. Check the layout on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
22. Preview the landing page.
23. Publish the page using the free Framer subdomain.
24. Share the landing page URL with your audience.
---
### Prompt for Framer AI
```commandline
Design a modern one-page landing page for a Hindi comedy book called "पंटर लोगों की टोली". The goal is to sell the book. Style should be bold, colorful, playful, and minimal with yellow, red, white, and black. Use large typography, comic-inspired accents, rounded cards, emoji illustrations, and a floating 3D book mockup. Include Hero, About, Why Read, Characters, Reviews, FAQ, CTA, and Footer. Hero headline: "हर मोहल्ले में होती है... एक पंटर लोगों की टोली!" Primary CTA: Buy Now. Mobile-first, responsive, clean spacing, smooth animations, and conversion-focused.
```
